# Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive business analysis dashboard created using Power BI. The objective is to analyze sales, profitability, customer segments, shipping modes, and regional performance and communicate key business insights through data visualization.

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand:

- Overall sales and profitability performance
- Sales performance across categories and sub-categories
- Profitability across categories, sub-categories, and regions
- Performance across customer segments and shipping modes
- Areas that require further business attention

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Business Overview
Provides a high-level view of total sales, total profit, total quantity, average discount, sales by category, and sales by region.

### 2. Sales Performance
Analyzes sales performance across sub-categories, customer segments, and shipping modes.

### 3. Profitability Analysis
Examines profit across categories, sub-categories, and regions, along with the relationship between discount and profit.

### 4. Focus Areas
Highlights profitability differences across states, customer segments, and shipping modes to identify areas requiring further attention.

### 5. Recommendations
Summarizes the major findings and provides business recommendations based on the analysis.

## Key Insights

- Sales performance varies across categories and sub-categories.
- Profitability differs significantly across products and regions.
- High sales do not always result in high profitability.
- Discount levels should be reviewed carefully to protect profit margins.
- Underperforming states and regions can be prioritized for deeper analysis.

## Recommendations

- Strengthen high-sales categories and sub-categories.
- Investigate low-profit and loss-making areas.
- Review discount strategies to balance sales and profitability.
- Focus on underperforming states and regions.
